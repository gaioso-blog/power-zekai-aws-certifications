# AIF-C01 — AWS Certified AI Practitioner (Foundational)

> Helper study map. The official exam guide is always the primary source of truth.
> Never claim a service is in scope unless the guide supports it.
>
> **Do not confuse with AIP-C01.** AIF-C01 is Foundational (AI Practitioner).
> AIP-C01 is Professional (Generative AI Developer).

## Main focus

Foundational AI, ML, generative AI, responsible AI, and AWS AI services.

## Common service families

- **AI services:** Amazon Bedrock, Amazon Q, Amazon Comprehend, Amazon Transcribe, Amazon Translate, Amazon Textract, Amazon Rekognition, Amazon Lex, Amazon Polly, Amazon Kendra
- **ML services:** Amazon SageMaker, SageMaker Canvas
- **Responsible AI:** bias, explainability, hallucination, toxicity, privacy, security, governance

## Question style

Prefer conceptual and service-selection questions involving AI versus ML versus generative AI, foundation models, prompt engineering, RAG, embeddings, vector stores, responsible AI, and selecting managed AI services.

## Common traps

- Confusing Textract (document text and structure) with Comprehend (language understanding)
- Confusing Transcribe (speech to text) with Polly (text to speech)
- Assuming fine-tuning is required when prompt engineering or RAG is sufficient
- Confusing supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning at the concept level
- Treating a foundation model as inherently free of bias or hallucination
- Confusing Amazon Q (assistant) with Bedrock (model access and building blocks)
- Assuming higher model size always means better fit for the stated requirement
